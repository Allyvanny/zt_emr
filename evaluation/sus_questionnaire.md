## Scoring

Standard SUS scoring:

- **Odd-numbered items (1,3,5,7,9):** score = response − 1
- **Even-numbered items (2,4,6,8,10):** score = 5 − response
- **Total:** sum all 10 adjusted scores, then **× 2.5** → SUS score out of 100

| SUS Score | Grade | Interpretation |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| 85 – 100  | A     | Excellent       |
| 70 – 84   | B     | Good            |
| 50 – 69   | C     | OK / acceptable |
| < 50      | D/F   | Poor            |
